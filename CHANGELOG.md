# Changelog

All notable changes to this project are documented here. The project follows [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org).

## 1.4.0 – 2026-09-26

- Clean line icons (Lucide) instead of emojis on buttons and options; the 🔥 logo and the pizza comparison stay

## 1.3.3 – 2026-09-26

- The quick start price is as large as the meeting counter

## 1.3.2 – 2026-09-26

- Faster, calmer app launch: a glowing flame on the right background instead of a black or white screen, and the cached app after 3 seconds on a slow network

## 1.3.1 – 2026-09-26

- Link previews: shared links show an image and description in Slack, Teams, WhatsApp and co.

## 1.3.0 – 2026-09-26

- Installable as an app (home screen icon, full screen without browser bar)
- Works offline after the first visit

## 1.2.0 – 2026-09-26

- The screen stays on while a meeting runs (Screen Wake Lock), and turns off normally when paused or ended

## 1.1.1 – 2026-09-26

- Larger meter counter on phones: short and medium amounts use up to ~40% more of the screen width

## 1.1.0 – 2026-09-26

- Share the result of a meeting as an image and text (share sheet on phones, download and clipboard on desktop)
- The app is online at https://ubongfx.github.io/burn-rate-meter/ and deploys automatically from main

## 1.0.0 – 2026-09-26

First release.

- Live meeting cost meter with falling bills, pause/resume and people joining or leaving mid-meeting
- Roles with hourly rates based on average German salaries; presets and default attendance per role
- Result card comparing the cost to pizzas, laptops and more
- German and English, light and dark mode, detected automatically
- Settings saved in the browser and synced between open tabs
- Works on phones, keyboard-accessible, WCAG 2.1 AA contrast
- Unit tests, end-to-end tests in Chromium and Firefox, accessibility scans and CI
